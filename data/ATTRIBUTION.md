# Vocabulary data attribution

## Collins Star Vocabulary

- Source: https://github.com/cd155/collins-vocabulary
- Upstream dictionary: https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT
- License: MIT
- Included fields: word, phonetic, Chinese translation, Collins star level,
  exam tags, and BNC frequency rank.

## Computer English Learning

- Source: https://github.com/kuanbandu/Computer-English-Learning
- License: MIT as declared by the source repository README.
- Included fields: English term, Chinese translation, pronunciation, example,
  and category.

The compact JSON files in this directory are generated from those sources by
`scripts/build-vocab-data.mjs`.
